Malice's Pits & Pendulums

A Charming Assortment of Entirely Avoidable Disasters.

For use with the 2024 revision of the Dungeons & Dragons fifth edition core rules

Malice the Clever, this plane's greatest designer of traps, shares a sampling of her most devious designs. Build them into your dungeon keeps and treasure houses to lure unwitting thieves to any number of demises, all while your true treasure stays safely hidden. Each trap comes with clear ways to detect, disarm, or destroy it, so clever players always have a fighting chance.

  • Wrecking Bhaal: Step on the wrong plate and a giant metal skull drops from the ceiling, then swings wildly around the sealed chamber.
  • Upbliette: A shaft of reversed gravity that flings intruders up to the ceiling and pins them there.
  • Whack-A-Troll: Clockwork trolls pop up from their pits and slash at anyone foolish enough to get close.
  • Wind-Up Colossus: A giant clockwork toy that stomps around the room, spewing sparks.
  • And more: A claw crane, a delirium-inducing censer, invisible swinging blades, and a slithering mosaic snake.

I Saw Goody Ann Dancing with the Devil

A One-Page RPG of Witch Trials and Wicked Accusations

4 to 6 players · Pens and scrap paper

Superstitious villagers stand trial for witchcraft, and everyone at the table is guilty of something.

  • Point the Finger: Each turn, one player is the Accused and everyone else writes a secret accusation of witchcraft. ("I saw Ann turn goat milk to blood!")
  • Vote for the Most Wicked: The Accused reads every accusation aloud, the table votes, and the best one earns its writer a Hunt point.
  • Confess or Deny: It doesn't matter. You're a witch either way.
  • Only One Survives: The player with the most Hunt points is crowned Witchfinder General. Everyone else picks their grisly end: hanged, drowned, burned, or crushed under heavy rocks (one crushing per game).

Free One-Page RPG

Wizard Apocalypse

Quarreling wizards wander a post-apocalyptic wasteland blighted by their own magic.

About 30 minutes · A pen and 7 homemade Spell cards per player

An ultra-light, one-page game of quick, chaotic fun. Learn it in five minutes:

  • Name Your Wizard: Invent the most ridiculous wizard name you can, then dream up the magical Blights plaguing the land.
  • Duel to the Death: Challenge a rival to a rock-paper-scissors-style spell duel with Chill, Grease, Imp, Potion, Shield, Torch, and Web.
  • Mind Your Head: Chant "For the right to fight the blight!" and reveal your Spell. Lose three times and your head explodes.

About Us

Wonderstone Workshop is a small, independent tabletop RPG studio making games that are easy to pick up and hard to forget.

Ross Wade

Writer & Lead Game Designer

Ross is a New York City animation writer for shows including P. King Duckling (Disney Junior), Super Wings (Universal Kids), and The Grossery Gang. His musical Scheme of the Super Bullies was part of the New York Children's Theater Festival. Most Wonderstone games begin with Ross.

Taylor Pavlik

Writer & Game Designer

Taylor is the managing editor of the literary magazine Midnight Breakfast and a project manager at the GRACE Communications Foundation, with more than a decade in print and digital media. He's the reason our wildest ideas actually make it to the table, on time.

David Gagne

Creative Director & Artist

David is a Senior Graphic Designer at Give Lively and runs a freelance design business at dgagne.com. He's also a music producer who makes music as Fantazmz and plays weekly at The Skillman in Sunnyside, Queens.
